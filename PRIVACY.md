# Privacy Policy

English | [Türkçe](PRIVACY.tr.md)

Pasteur does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## What is stored

- **Settings:** your options (enabled state, notification position, search engine, excluded sites and similar) are saved with the browser's sync storage (`storage.sync`). If you use Firefox Sync, the browser may sync these settings across your devices, as it does for other browser settings.
- **Highlights:** the text you highlight, with a short piece of the text around it, is saved on your device only (`storage.local`), per page address. It never leaves your browser.

## What is sent

- Pasteur makes no network requests of its own. There is no server, no analytics and no telemetry.
- The search button opens your chosen search engine (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Yandex) in a new tab with the selected text, only when you click it. That search is governed by the search engine's own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **Clipboard write:** to copy the selected text or link when you ask for it.
- **Storage:** to save the settings and highlights described above.
- **Active tab and content scripts on all pages:** to show the notification where you select text.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through an [issue](https://github.com/aybrkaknc/pasteur/issues/new/choose) on the project repository.
